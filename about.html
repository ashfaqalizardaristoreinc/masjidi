About Masjidi

A small app with a big mission — helping the Ummah stay connected to the masjid.

Masjidi was born out of a simple frustration: when travelling to a new city, finding a nearby mosque with accurate prayer and Jama'ah timings could take more effort than it should. Most apps were cluttered with ads, required sign-ups, or simply didn't have good local coverage.

We built Masjidi to solve that — a fast, private, offline-first mosque finder that "just works". No accounts. No ads. No trackers. Your data stays on your device. And because we rely on OpenStreetMap and AlAdhan, we can serve accurate prayer times to millions of users across the world without charging a single rupee.

Our principles

🕌 Built for the Ummah

Free forever, for every Muslim, in every country.

🔒 Privacy first

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your data never leaves your device.

🌍 Community-driven

Mosque data and amenity tags are improved by users and Imams.

📴 Offline-first

Everything important works without an internet connection.

Built with open-source

Masjidi is proudly built on Flutter, Drift (SQLite), OpenStreetMap, Leaflet/MapLibre, Nominatim, AlAdhan API, AlQuran Cloud and other open tools. Wherever possible, we contribute back to the community.
