Masjidi

Your mosque companion

Everything you need

Built for the Ummah, designed for you

From finding the nearest masjid to knowing exactly when the Jama'ah starts — Masjidi handles it all.

Live GPS & Map

Instantly detect your location and see every mosque, musalla, and Islamic center on an interactive OpenStreetMap.

Prayer & Jama'ah Times

Accurate 5-time daily schedule with Hanafi, Shafi'i, and other juristic methods — plus live countdowns.

Qibla Compass

Precise GPS-and-compass Qibla direction with distance to the Kaaba, works anywhere in the world.

Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Get guided to the mosque entrance using Google Maps or your default navigation app.

Verified Amenities

Community-verified tags for parking, wudu areas, wheelchair access, and women's sections.

Daily Quran & Hadith

Beautiful daily verses and authentic hadiths with multi-language sharing options.

Widgets & Notifications

Home-screen and lock-screen widgets plus gentle prayer-time notifications.

100% Offline Capable

Your mosque data and prayer times are stored locally using SQLite — no internet required.
