Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's exactly what Masjidi does — and does not — do.

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Masjidi ("the App") — developed by Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store ("we", "us") — handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and have designed Masjidi to be privacy-first by default.

In short: Masjidi does not collect any personal information. We use no ads, no analytics, and no tracking SDKs. All mosque data, prayer times, and your favourites are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Location data

With your explicit permission, Masjidi accesses your device's precise and/or approximate location to:

  • Show nearby mosques, musallas, and Islamic centers on the map.
  • Calculate accurate prayer times for your current position.
  • Determine the Qibla direction and distance to the Kaaba.

Location data is processed only on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers (we don't operate any) and never shared with third parties except the public APIs listed in section 3, which receive only the minimum coordinates needed to return a result.

Data you create

Favourite mosques, saved settings, prayer-time adjustments, admin credentials, and any crowdsourced edits you submit are stored locally on your device using SQLite (via the Drift library). You can export or delete this data at any time from within the App.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (we never ask).
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or messages.
  • Your advertising ID.
  • Crash reports or analytics (we use no such SDKs).
  • Any form of account or login data (except the locally stored admin password you set yourself, which never leaves your device).

2. Permissions We Request

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — to find nearby mosques and calculate prayer times.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to send prayer-time reminders (opt-in).
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to deliver prayer notifications on time.
  • INTERNET — only to fetch map tiles, mosque data, prayer times, Quranic content, and to open navigation links.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time from your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services

Masjidi uses the following public APIs. Each may receive your approximate coordinates, IP address, and standard request headers when the App makes a request on your behalf:

  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) — map tiles. Privacy policy.
  • Overpass API (overpass-api.de) — fetches mosque locations within a radius.
  • Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) — reverse-geocodes coordinates into addresses.
  • AlAdhan API (api.aladhan.com) — prayer times and Qibla calculations. Privacy policy.
  • AlQuran Cloud API (api.alquran.cloud) — daily Quranic verses.

We do not share any data with these services other than the minimum required for the request (typically latitude/longitude).

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All user-created data lives on your device. Uninstalling Masjidi removes all of it. To delete individual items, use the admin panel or the relevant screen within the App.

5. Children's Privacy

Masjidi is not directed to children under 13. Because we collect no personal data, no special handling is required, but we encourage parental supervision for users under 13.

6. Security

Admin credentials in the App are stored using flutter_secure_storage, backed by the platform's Keychain (iOS) or EncryptedSharedPreferences (Android). Prayer times, mosque data, and favourites are stored in a plain SQLite database on your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the App and on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
