Support & Help

We're here to help you get the most out of Masjidi.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find mosques near me?

Open the app and allow location permission. The map will automatically show all nearby mosques within a 5 km radius. You can expand the radius or filter by distance in Settings.

Are the prayer times accurate?

Prayer times are calculated using the AlAdhan API, which is a widely trusted source. Times may differ slightly from your local mosque's announcements because of calculation methods and Juristic school settings. You can change these in Settings → Calculation Method.

How do I add a missing mosque?

From the map screen, tap the "+" button and fill in the mosque details. Logged-in admins can add directly; regular users can submit suggestions that will be reviewed.

Can I use the app without internet?

Yes! Mosque data and prayer times are stored locally using SQLite. Map tiles are cached for offline viewing. You'll need internet only for the initial mosque download and for live Qibla compass calibration.

The Qibla compass is not working — what should I do?

Your device likely needs to be calibrated. Move your phone in a figure-8 pattern a few times to calibrate the magnetometer, then re-open the Qibla screen. Some devices without a magnetometer won't support the compass — in that case, use the numerical degree value displayed.

How do I change the theme or language?

Go to Settings → Theme to pick Light, Dark, or System Default. Language options (English, Urdu, Arabic) are available in Settings → Language.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. Masjidi collects no personal data, uses no ads, and stores everything locally on your device. Read our full Privacy Policy.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us at support@ashfaqalizardaristore.co with a clear description (and screenshots if possible). We read every email.
